## 3. Concept Papers

Follow these steps every time you create a concept paper:

1. Open the new format template: <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fgbH2xUa-ootAed6tqUmW3ZaPiymki2I/edit#heading=h.4a472bhphip3>
   - The first page of this format must be filled out every time you make a concept paper.
2. Study the sample concept papers in the Google Drive under the **CONCEPT PAPERS** folder. Go through each section — they are easy to follow. The only part that changes between papers is the header.
3. Write your content. You can use AI tools for the descriptions, but fix the dates, venue, and other event details yourself.
4. For the budget, base it on the bank book.

**Scheduling rule:** event dates must **not** fall within 1 week before exam week or during exam week itself. You only have a ~2-week window in between — plan around it.

**International webinars** (e.g., Sir Jafaar, Clint Erno) require an **RFP** on top of the normal paperwork — it adds one signatory. Ask Ma'am Feb for the current RFP process.

**Checklist — complete all of these before asking the School Director to sign:**

- [ ] Summary letter
- [ ] Board resolution
- [ ] Concept paper
- [ ] Speaker's CV
- [ ] Speaker's certificates
- [ ] Justification letter explaining why you're buying from an outside supplier instead of the school canteen (when applicable)

## 9. Venue Reservation

**Golden rule: finish the concept papers first, then reserve the venue through the GSD (General Services Department).** Go to the GSD in person — do not call around asking random people.

- Target: have the papers done **1 month before the event**. This is the lesson from Plug Into The Future — by the time the paper was ready, October was already fully booked.

**New gym and old gym:**

- You can ask **Coach** for a pencil booking (a temporary hold) if there are still open slots on his calendar.
- His office is in the varsity room — across from the bookstore, beside the clinic and the POD.
- A pencil booking only becomes confirmed if the paper is done **within 3 days**. After that, the hold lapses.

**IHM venue:**

- First come, first served — there are no pencil bookings for IHM.
- Always check with the GSD.

## 12. Outside Events

Complete this checklist for any event held outside campus:

- [ ] **CHED letter** — the official format is in **CHED CMO No. 63 s. 2017, pages 14–16**: <https://ofa.upd.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/CHED-CMO-No-63-s.-2017-Policies-and-Guidelines-on-Local-Off-Campus-Activity.pdf> (per a CBA informant — follow that letter format). The SSC letter template also works — just change the header to CCS: <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wDysZkC9mNEkij-JhcjbrOQVfXw2zsORi_KnuVPZIsU/edit?usp=sharing>
- [ ] **Deadline: submit at least 15 days before the event — but target ~1 month ahead.** Processing at the City Hall Compound (Jose P. Laurel Highway, Barangay Marawoy, Lipa City) is slow.
- [ ] **Submission is two-channel:** a **hard copy** must reach the CHED office **and** a copy must be **emailed** to them. Get CHED's email address from **Ma'am Lily at the Registrar** — the letter may need to be coursed through the Registrar anyway.
- [ ] **Participant list + parents' consent** — list every student joining and secure parental consent for each.
- [ ] **Curriculum forms** — one per course (IT and CS), with the subjects relevant to the activity highlighted.
- [ ] **Medical checkup letter** — signatory is **Ma'am Vincoy**; write the letter focused on the clinic checkup for participants. **Ma'am Feb** can confirm who it should be addressed to / how it's routed. Schedule the actual checkup with the clinic.
- [ ] **Van request** — get the form from the GSD. Adapt the CBA reference letter (they used a bus — revise it to say van). CBA's outside-activity papers for reference: <https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HgvijpHLqAfF_c2IhGCnYD9AUUT7Vd0J?usp=sharing>

**Why it seemed like no CHED letter was needed before:** the hard copy was never actually sent to the CHED office — it was only emailed. The letter is still required for competitions and all other outside events.

**Exception:** CES activities are the only outside events that do not need a CHED letter (this has happened before, though the reason isn't documented).

**Not sure about the process?** Ask directly at SAS (Ma'am Ana) or ask SSC President Cez.
