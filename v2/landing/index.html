Everything the org runs on

Duty & journal

A weekly duty roster everyone can see, and a daily journal that files itself into attendance — no more end-of-semester reconstruction.

Papers that move

Register a letter or concept paper and CounciLog knows which desks it visits, in order. Sign, skip, revise, or log where it physically is.

Projects & checklists

Every event generates its checklist from your org's templates — venue bookings, food, equipment — with due dates counted back from the event.

Templates & chains

Teach CounciLog your routes once. New papers pick the right chain automatically, and conditional steps only appear when they apply.

Members & roles

Invite by link or approve join requests, assign positions, and keep the audit log append-only — who changed what is never a mystery.

Your org, your rules

Every org configures its own chains, templates, duty days, and contacts. Nothing is hardcoded to one council's workflow.
