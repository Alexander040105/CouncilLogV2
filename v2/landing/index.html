Why "CounciLog"?

Council + log. Say it fast and it sounds like silog — the Filipino breakfast that's a complete meal on one plate: rice, egg, and a protein, nothing extra to order. Same idea here: roster, journal, papers, budgets, checklists — one plate, no side orders.

It's also the official meal of the 2 AM deadline. If you've ever finished a letter the night before it was due, you get it.

The full plate

Duty & journal

A weekly duty roster everyone can see, and a daily journal that files itself into attendance — no end-of-semester archaeology.

Papers that move

Register a letter or concept paper and CounciLog knows which desks it visits, in order. "Who has it?" finally has an answer.

Projects & checklists

Every event generates its checklist from your org's templates — venue bookings, food, equipment — with due dates counted back from the event.

Templates & chains

Teach CounciLog your routes once. New papers pick the right chain automatically, and conditional steps only appear when they apply.

Members & roles

Invite by link or approve join requests, assign positions, and keep the audit log append-only — who changed what is never a mystery.

Budget & receipts

The bankbook tracks the council's balance. Every event gets a budget backed by its board resolution, receipts attach to each expense, and the financial report practically writes itself.

Your org, your rules

Every org configures its own chains, templates, duty days, and contacts. Nothing is hardcoded to one council's workflow.

On iPhone?

There's no iPhone app yet — and there doesn't need to be. Open the web app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It sits on your home screen and opens full-screen like an app — same account, same data.
